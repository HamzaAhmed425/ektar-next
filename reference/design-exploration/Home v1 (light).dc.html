The problem

Banking fraud has changed. Most defences haven't.

When customers log into their banking app, they expect branch-level security. Mobile apps, internet banking portals, and payment APIs have created a fundamentally different attack surface — one fraudsters can probe from anywhere, at scale, at near-zero cost. The tools most banks rely on were built for a different era. The threat has moved on.
