ekProtect · Protect the Device & App

Attest the device. Read the behaviour. Kill the session.

ekProtect embeds in the banking app. It attests device and app integrity, detects overlay attacks, malware, rooted devices, and remote access tools in real time, and suspends the session automatically when a threat is found. Every signal feeds into your risk decisioning — your existing system, or ekRules.

Capabilities

What ekProtect detects and stops

01

Device & app attestation

Confirms the app is genuine and unmodified, and the device is in a state the bank can trust.

02

Overlay attacks & basic malware detection

Detects fake screens drawn over the real app and flags malicious code running alongside it, from inside the app itself.

03

Rooted and jailbroken devices

Compromised operating systems are identified before a session is trusted.

04

Remote access tools (RATs)

Detects sessions being driven remotely while the customer watches.

05

Automatic session suspension

When a threat is found the session is suspended automatically — no manual review in the path.
